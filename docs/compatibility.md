# PBO Compatibility

PBO feature availability depends on the **processor, motherboard, BIOS/AGESA version, and system manufacturer**.

## Before you begin

Confirm that your processor and motherboard firmware support the PBO features you intend to use, your cooling is adequate, and you know how to recover or clear CMOS.

## Ryzen generations

This guide is intended to cover supported desktop Ryzen families across AM4 and AM5. Feature availability differs across Ryzen 3000, 5000, X3D, 7000, 8000G, 9000, and newer supported products. Some older processors/APUs may not support PBO, and OEM systems may hide the controls.

## X3D processors

Treat X3D processors separately from non-X3D models. Voltage, boost, Curve Optimizer, and related controls may be restricted or behave differently depending on generation and firmware.

## How to check

1. Identify the exact CPU.
2. Identify the exact motherboard or prebuilt model.
3. Record the BIOS version.
4. Check manufacturer documentation.
5. Enter UEFI/BIOS and verify which PBO controls are actually present.
