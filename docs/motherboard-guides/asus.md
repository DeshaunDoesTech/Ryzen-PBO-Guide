# ASUS PBO BIOS Guide

> Menu names vary by motherboard generation and BIOS version.

Look for PBO controls in areas such as **AMD Overclocking** and ASUS CPU/overclocking menus. Record your BIOS version, current settings, and Clear CMOS procedure before tuning. If multiple PBO menus appear, do not change duplicate controls blindly.
