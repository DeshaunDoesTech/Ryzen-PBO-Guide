# PBO Basics

PBO works with Ryzen's automatic boost behavior rather than replacing it with a fixed clock.

## Common controls

Depending on CPU and firmware, you may see PBO enable/disable/advanced modes, PPT/TDC/EDC, motherboard limits, Curve Optimizer, Max CPU Boost Clock Override, Scalar, and thermal limits.

Not every control is available on every Ryzen processor.

## More power is not always more performance

Ryzen boosting is constrained by several variables simultaneously. Increasing one limit may do nothing when temperature, frequency, voltage, another electrical limit, or workload behavior is the actual constraint.

The goal is to identify the limiting factor rather than maximize every number.
